How we work

Coaching, not legal or financial advice

Veyylora coaches the conversation: preparation, framing, delivery and nerve. We're not lawyers and not financial advisers, and we don't review contracts as legal work — for terms, obligations and anything with legal consequence, use an attorney.

We also won't make the decision for you. Whether to accept an offer, walk away or hold a price is yours; our job is to make sure you go in prepared and come out knowing what happened.

Frequently asked

The questions we get most

Do I have to be aggressive?

No, and aggression usually costs money. Preparation, clear numbers and comfortable silence do far more work than pressure.

They asked my salary expectations first.

A specific situation with good answers depending on your state's laws and your position. We rehearse it, because improvising costs people thousands.

Can you review my contract?

Not as legal advice. We'll help you think about what to ask for and how to raise it; the terms themselves need an attorney.

What if they say no?

Often the real conversation starts there — what would need to change, by when, and what's negotiable besides money. That's a whole section of the salary program.

I freeze up in these conversations.

Rehearsal is the fix. Saying the number out loud a dozen times to a coach makes the real moment far less charged.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. No contract, no minimum term. Access stays open until the end of the month you already paid for.

Got a conversation coming up?

Twenty minutes on the situation, your numbers and your first ask. Free.
