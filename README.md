## Business description (Stripe, directories…)

```
Coaching in negotiation, delivered online: one-on-one video sessions and small group sessions covering preparation and framing, understanding your numbers and alternatives, salary and compensation conversations, and client, vendor and contract discussions. Clients follow an 8 to 12 week program with rehearsal of their real negotiations and a written plan for each one. We are not lawyers or financial advisers and give no legal or financial advice. Services are sold as month-to-month subscriptions from $49 to $239 per month, cancellable at any time. No physical products are sold or shipped. Site: veyylora.shop
```
